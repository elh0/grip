Most climbers film everything and watch nothing back. Grip watches it for you. It finds the moments that cost you the send and tells you what to try next session, in plain words.

Film

Prop it up and climb.

No selfie stick, no friend holding it. Lean the phone on your bag at 0.5×, line the start and top hold up inside the box, and go.

Tap the record button on the phone.

Tag

Say where you were.

Gym, colour, grade and whether you sent it. Four taps, so your climbs line up by wall and you can see a grade come together over a month.

Pick a colour, then press Get my notes.

Notes

Three notes come back.

Not a score, not forty stats. The three moments worth fixing, each one on the frame it happened, with the joint angles that show why.

Tap a note to jump the video to it.

Rate

Tell it if it helped.

Every note has two buttons. What you tap shapes the next review, so after a few sessions the notes sound like your coach, not a textbook.

Try Helped or Not for me.

Train

Your notes turn into drills. Keep missing the heel? Tomorrow's session has heel-hook drills on the board, a 4×4 on the overhang and hamstring work to back it up.

Tick off a drill, then press Start.
